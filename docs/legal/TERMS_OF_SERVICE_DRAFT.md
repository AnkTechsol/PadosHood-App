# Terms of Service — review draft only

> **DRAFT ONLY — REQUIRES QUALIFIED LEGAL REVIEW AND SOCIETY/OPERATOR APPROVAL. NOT OFFICIAL TERMS AND MUST NOT BE PUBLISHED AS-IS.** Product operator, governing law, jurisdiction, contact, eligibility, liability allocation, and all enforceable language require owner and counsel decisions.

This working draft is intended to describe the planned Angaan service for Woodsville Phase 2. It is not a contract or legal advice.

## Service and access

The planned service provides society membership requests, committee-managed approval, notices, text discussions, and complaint handling. Authentication uses Clerk. A request to join does not guarantee approval or create administrator privileges. The society/operator must identify who may participate, when access may be suspended or terminated, how appeals/requests are handled, and how account closure interacts with the separate Clerk account.

## User-submitted material

Users may submit discussion posts and complaints; committee administrators may publish notices and add complaint timeline notes. The final terms must state permitted use, moderation grounds/process, user-content licensing (if any), ownership, copyright claims, and treatment of records after account deletion. Do not insert an AI-output license or AI terms: the launch scope has no AI feature.

## Service limitations and liability

Counsel must draft and approve any warranty disclaimers, availability statements, emergency-service disclaimer, limitation of liability, indemnity, or consumer disclosures applicable to this society and jurisdiction. No emergency response or service-level guarantee is implemented or promised by this draft.

## Termination, governing law, and notices

Counsel and the society/operator must determine account suspension/termination rules, complaint/contact process, governing law, venue, effective date, amendment notice, and who legally operates the service. Do not publish until those entries and the linked privacy/acceptable-use drafts are approved.

No legal conclusions or compliance claims are made. The gaps are tracked in [LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED.md](LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED.md).
