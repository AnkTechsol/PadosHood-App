# Legal Review Required

This is a checklist of unresolved owner/operator and qualified counsel decisions, not evidence of completed review. The following documents are drafts and are **not official published policies**:

- [ ] `PRIVACY_POLICY_DRAFT.md` — confirm legal operator, data categories, service providers/locations, applicable rights/bases, retention, backups, Clerk identity handling, contact and request process.
- [ ] `TERMS_OF_SERVICE_DRAFT.md` — define jurisdiction, eligibility, account approval/suspension/termination, user content/moderation, disclaimers/liability and effective notices.
- [ ] `ACCEPTABLE_USE_POLICY_DRAFT.md` — define moderation, complaint/reporting, enforcement, appeals and support channels.

Before real resident launch, the society/operator must also decide and document:

- [ ] Legal entity/operator authority and committee authorization for collecting/using resident information.
- [ ] Support/contact identity that may be published; do not substitute an invented contact.
- [ ] Retention periods and access controls for member records, notices, user posts, complaints, timeline notes, audit metadata, logs, and backups.
- [ ] Production Clerk and Replit accounts/terms, PostgreSQL host, data regions, subprocessors, backup policies, and any required notices/transfers.
- [ ] Process for data access/correction/deletion requests, including separate Clerk-account and backup handling.
- [ ] Counsel's review of jurisdiction-specific privacy, consumer, housing/society, recordkeeping, and user-generated-content obligations.

No new AI integration is approved or in initial scope. Any future AI or other vendor must receive a separate data-flow, risk, provider, and legal review before use. No legal compliance claim is made here.
