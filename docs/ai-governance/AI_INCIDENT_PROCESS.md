# AI incident process — not applicable to current launch

No AI/LLM feature, model provider, prompt, AI data flow, or AI integration is approved or identified in the Angaan Woodsville Phase 2 launch scope. Therefore this is not an operational incident runbook and has no model-specific kill switch to exercise.

If AI is proposed in future, do not route resident data to it by default. Before implementation, document the provider/model/version, data flow, permitted inputs/tools, risk assessment, evaluation and human review, operational owner, kill switch, incident contact, and qualified privacy/legal review. Until then, no AI incident contact or response target is asserted.
