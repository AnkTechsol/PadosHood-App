# Terms & Acceptable Use Skill

## 1. Purpose
Prepares lawyer-reviewable drafts of Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policies.

## 2. When to use
- New product launches, adding user-generated content features, or AI-specific features.

## 3. Required inputs
- Feature scope, user types, and AI capabilities.

## 4. Required workflow
1. Draft Terms of Service covering account termination, governing law placeholders, and limitation of liability.
2. Draft Acceptable Use Policy outlining prohibited uses.
3. Include AI-specific disclosures and user-content/IP clauses if applicable.
4. Flag heavily for legal counsel review.

## 5. Required outputs
- Terms of Service draft.
- Acceptable Use Policy draft.

## 6. Quality gates
- Does it cover AI-specific disclaimers?
- Is it clearly marked as a draft?

## 7. Common failure modes
- Playing lawyer and making definitive legal claims.
- Missing IP clauses when dealing with LLM outputs.

## 8. Completion checklist
- [ ] ToS drafted.
- [ ] AUP drafted.
- [ ] AI disclosures included.
- [ ] Marked for legal review.
