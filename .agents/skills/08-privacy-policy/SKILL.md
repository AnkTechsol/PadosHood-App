# Privacy Policy Drafting Skill

## 1. Purpose
Generates a draft privacy policy strictly based on collected product facts. Does NOT claim legal compliance.

## 2. When to use
- When a new product launches or data practices change significantly.

## 3. Required inputs
- Data inventory and subprocessor list.
- Company/Entity details and user types.

## 4. Required workflow
1. Collect verified product facts (data categories, purposes, vendors, AI usage).
2. Draft policy in clear language.
3. Generate plain-language summary.
4. Create lists for Missing Information and Legal Review Required.

## 5. Required outputs
- Privacy-policy draft.
- Missing-information list.
- "Legal review required" list.

## 6. Quality gates
- Does it accurately reflect the Data Inventory?
- Is it clear that this is a draft needing legal counsel?

## 7. Common failure modes
- Making unverified claims (e.g., claiming GDPR compliance).
- Missing third-party AI or analytics tools from the disclosure.

## 8. Completion checklist
- [ ] Facts collected.
- [ ] Draft generated.
- [ ] No legal compliance claims fabricated.
- [ ] Marked for legal review.
