# Approved launch scope

This older feature list conflicted with the owner's selected scope and described unapproved features as requirements. It is replaced by the authoritative [Woodsville Phase 2 launch scope](docs/product/SOCIETY_LAUNCH.md) and [API contract](docs/engineering/API.md).

The initial scope is limited to Clerk email authentication, resident membership requests and committee approval, notices, text-only discussion posts, private resident complaints with an admin status timeline, membership administration, and own-data export/application-account deletion. No attachments, comments, likes, read receipts, staff assignment, SLA promises, star feedback, or resident role-switching are in the launch contract.

Implementation and release status are tracked by the owning engineering work and release evidence; this page does not claim that the features are finished or verified.
